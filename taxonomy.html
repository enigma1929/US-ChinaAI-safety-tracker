Taxonomy

Every benchmark sits in exactly one category, decided by what it measures. In CBRN (chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear weapons), Cyber, and Autonomy and AI R&D, a benchmark is either a capability test or a refusal test. Safeguards holds refusal and robustness tests outside those three domains. The placement rules are on the Method page. ★ marks the 15 benchmarks reported for the most models.
